We're celebrating Hacktoberfest with **two powerful tracks** each designed to reward and showcase the true spirit of open source!

### **1. Contributor Track:**  
**Earn exclusive Akash swag and prizes for every contribution you make to our open-source repos!**  
- Browse [Akash Network's GitHub repositories](https://github.com/akash-network) for Hacktoberfest-labeled issues.
- Every **meaningful pull request** helps you climb our rewards ladder (T-shirt, gaming mouse, mechanical keyboard).
- All merged PRs count code, docs, templates, tools updating, security, or automation!

*Perfect if you want to get into open source, learn new skills, and give back to the decentralized infrastructure movement.*

***

### **2. Maintainer/Project Track:**  
**Showcase your project as a DePIN Pioneer!**  
- If you maintain or build a project (personally, in a team, or as a collective), enter it into the Akash Hacktoberfest Showcase.
- Your project must **actively use Akash as a cloud provider** that could be for hosting, compute, GPU tasks, decentralized dApps, or even as part of your workflow.
- The community will select standout projects that best demonstrate the real-world power of Akash Supercloud.
- Four winning projects will each receive **$100 Akash Compute Credits** and a **exclusive swag pack** for the main maintainer plus global spotlight across Akash's channels.

*This is the perfect chance to advocate for your project, incentivize your OSS community, and inspire new builders to try Akash!*

***

**In summary:**  
- **Contributors:** Hacktoberfest-style PRs on Akash repos = instant rewards & learning  
- **Maintainers/Projects:** Bring a project that uses Akash, inspire the ecosystem, and win big for innovation
